ROM Revver — a live RX-8 workspace

Free, open tuning tools proved what's possible. ROM Revver's mission is to give that community a calibration editor that feels as good as the cars it tunes — quick to navigate, genuinely pleasant to use, and safe by construction.

It reads RomRaider- and ECUFlash-format definitions, so your existing knowledge and files come with you. Reading and flashing stay with the community's proven tools — ROM Revver is the editor in the middle, reimagined.

Built for real tuning

Everything you need to shape a calibration

Dense, fast, and visual — with the safety rails that keep a flash correct.

⚡

Fast table editing

Select, type, increment, interpolate, and paste across a dense, searchable table tree. Full undo/redo on every change.

🧊

2D & 3D visualization

See any map as a scaled grid, a heatmap, or an interactive 3D surface — and watch it update live as you edit.

🔀

Compare & diff

Load other ROMs as baselines, see per-cell deltas inline, and copy values straight from a comparison — byte-exact.

🛡️

Safe by construction

Your original file is never touched. Bytes are never written without a recalculated, verified checksum. Every edit is reversible.

🧩

Plugins, no code

Extend the app with declarative plugins — panels, notes, and reversible, previewable mods. Nothing runs silently.

🚗

Multi-platform

Mazda RX-8 today; Mitsubishi Evo support built in; Nissan on the way. You supply the definitions — they're never bundled.
